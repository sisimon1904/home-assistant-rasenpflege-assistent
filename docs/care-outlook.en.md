# Care outlook and comparison diagnostics

The care plan adds `outlook` for **48 elapsed hours**, including daylight-saving transitions. It combines existing priorities with cached mowing windows and the next automatic irrigation start. Without a controlled valve, the cached watering weather window provides manual guidance. `candidate_at` is a time to review; `not_before` remains unknown while a predecessor still needs to complete. Watering, drying and mowing are not reserved as a fixed chain. Fertilizing remains unscheduled without a supported clock time. Frost and missing inputs retain their existing blockers. The preview does not simulate future soil moisture or execute actions.

`retrospective.summary` separately counts saved windows with suitable sampled weather, observed blockers and insufficient evidence. Identical duplicate windows cannot inflate counts. Overlapping advice may share weather samples, so no accuracy score or confirmed grass dryness is reported.

`quantity_comparison` in irrigation status and readiness reviews up to 20 recent session records from the last 30 days. Only fully measured targeted sessions with matching area, valve/meter sources and meter semantics are comparable. Gaps, flow estimates, changed sources and unknown legacy data are counted separately. The descriptive tolerance is **5% of the target or 1 liter, whichever is larger**. Stopped shortfalls are distinguished from sessions completed as target reached. At least three comparable overshoots or target-reached shortfalls produce a review hint, not a confirmed fault or an automatic settings/dose adjustment. Older records without comparison metadata remain in consumption history.

`model_insights.soil_evidence` reports the **currently valid** soil reading and evidence basis. A sensor does not replace the model: an eligible correction adopts 25% of the difference from the reading. Reusing one report cannot accumulate repeated corrections. Stale or invalid sensors restore model-only estimates without forcing moisture to zero. Tests check analytic thirty-day dry-down, root depths, storms with slope/compaction, repeated water additions and sensor outages. This does not replace local field validation.

The [care-plan card](dashboard/care-plan.en.yaml) shows the preview; the [diagnostics card](dashboard/diagnostics.en.yaml) shows review counts, target comparisons and soil evidence. No new entities or extra regular weather requests are introduced.
